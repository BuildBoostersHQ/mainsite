Why Speed-to-Lead Matters More Than Your Ad Budget

Contractors who respond to leads in under 5 minutes book 3x more jobs. Here's why your response time matters more than how much you spend on ads.

By Madeleine JolieSeptember 28, 20265 min read

I talk to contractors every week who spend $2,000 or $3,000 a month on Google Ads and wonder why the phone rings but jobs don't close. Nine times out of ten, the problem isn't the ad. The problem is what happens after someone fills out the form.

Here's a number that changed how I think about contractor marketing: leads contacted within five minutes are 21 times more likely to convert than leads contacted after 30 minutes. That stat comes from a study by Lead Connect, and I've watched it play out with every contractor I've worked with.

What actually happens when a lead waits

A homeowner needs their roof looked at. They Google "roofer near me," click the first three results, and fill out a quote form on each one. That took about four minutes.

The first contractor to call back wins. Period.

By the time you check your email at lunch and see the lead from 9 AM, that homeowner has already talked to someone else, liked them fine, and scheduled an estimate. Your $47 Google click just paid for someone else's job.

The math behind response time

Say you're spending $1,500 a month on ads and getting 60 leads. You close 8 of them. That's a 13% close rate and roughly $187 per booked job in ad spend.

Now keep that same $1,500 budget but respond to every lead within two minutes using an automated text. Even a conservative bump to 15 or 16 closed jobs cuts your cost per job in half. You didn't spend a single extra dollar on advertising. You just answered faster.

What "fast" looks like for a contractor

You're on a roof. You're under a house. You're driving. You can't answer every call and text the second it comes in. I get it. That's why the system has to handle the first touch for you.

When a lead comes in through your website, they should get a text back within 60 seconds. Something like: "Hey, this is [name] with [company]. Got your request. When's a good time to talk?"

That text does three things. It confirms you're real and paying attention. It starts the conversation before your competitor does. And it buys you time to call back when you're off the ladder.

The cold truth about "I'll call them tonight"

I've heard this from dozens of contractors. "I write down the leads and call them when I get home." By 7 PM, that homeowner has forgotten they even filled out your form. Or worse, they remember and they're annoyed nobody called.

The contractors I work with who automated their first response saw their booking rate jump 30 to 40 percent without changing anything else about their business. Same ads, same website, same crew. Just faster follow-up.

What to do right now

If you're running any kind of advertising or even just getting organic leads from Google, check your response time. Have someone fill out your form and time how long it takes to hear back. If the answer is "whenever I see it," you're losing jobs every single day.

Automated missed-call text-back and instant lead response aren't fancy extras. For a contractor, they're the difference between a $200 cost per job and a $400 one.
