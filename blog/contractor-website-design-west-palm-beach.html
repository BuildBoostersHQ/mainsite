Websites and Lead Systems for West Palm Beach Contractors

Palm Beach County is one of the wealthiest counties in Florida and one of the most competitive markets for contractors. Homeowners in Jupiter, Boca Raton, and Delray Beach expect quality work, fast communication, and professionalism from the first contact. The contractors winning in this market aren't always the cheapest. They're the ones who look professional online and respond before anyone else.

Build Boosters builds websites and lead systems for contractors in West Palm Beach, Boca Raton, Boynton Beach, and across Palm Beach County. We work with roofers, plumbers, HVAC companies, electricians, painters, remodelers, landscapers, pool builders, and pressure washing crews.

What Makes Palm Beach County Different

Average home values in Palm Beach County run significantly higher than the state average. That means higher project values for contractors, but also higher expectations from homeowners. A homeowner in Jupiter spending $40,000 on a kitchen remodel is going to research contractors thoroughly before they call anyone.

They'll look at your website, read your reviews, check your photos, and compare you against three or four competitors. If your website looks like it was built in 2018, has stock photos, and says "quality craftsmanship" without showing any examples, you lose to the competitor whose site has a gallery of real projects, 120 Google reviews, and a quote form that takes 30 seconds to fill out.

We build sites that hold up under that level of scrutiny. Real photos, real reviews, service-specific pages with enough detail to answer the homeowner's questions before they pick up the phone.

What We Build for Palm Beach County Contractors

Websites that match your market. We build multi-page contractor websites targeting West Palm Beach, Boca Raton, Jupiter, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, Lake Worth, Wellington, and Royal Palm Beach individually. Each location page targets the searches homeowners in that specific city make.

Automated lead follow-up. Speed-to-lead matters everywhere, but it matters more in a market where homeowners have plenty of options and high expectations. A lead who fills out your form and doesn't hear back for two hours has already contacted someone else. Our system texts them within 60 seconds and starts the conversation for you.

Review marketing. Palm Beach County homeowners read reviews carefully. The difference between 30 reviews and 130 reviews is the difference between "maybe" and "I'm calling them." Automated review requests go out after every job. Contractors using this system consistently add 10 to 15 new reviews per month.

Google Business Profile optimization. We optimize your GBP for the specific cities and service categories that match your business. Palm Beach County stretches from Boca Raton to Jupiter, over 50 miles of coastline with distinct communities. Your GBP service area needs to reflect exactly where you work.
