About · Author

Madeleine Jolie

Founder of Build Boosters and the person who writes every article on this blog.

I'm Maddie, and I started Build Boosters because I kept seeing good contractors lose jobs to slower, less careful competitors who simply answered the phone first or had more reviews on Google.

I grew up around my grandfather's carpet store in Brooklyn, so I learned early that a business like yours runs on early mornings, hard work and customers who call back because you did the job right. Before Build Boosters, I worked in hospitality at the Ritz-Carlton and then at GoHighLevel, the software a lot of marketing agencies use to run websites, texting and follow-up for small businesses, where I worked closely with the agency owners building those systems.

Build Boosters takes the same kind of tools and sets them up for contractors who don't have time to deal with them, so you get a website that brings in calls, an automatic text when you can't pick up, and review requests that go out after every job without you having to remember.

I write these articles the way I'd explain things to a contractor over the phone, with real numbers where I can find them and links to where they came from. If something I wrote doesn't match what you see in your business, I'd honestly like to hear about it, so call or text me at (786) 479-8856.
