This is an estimate, not a promise. It assumes every missed call is a real customer who would have closed at your usual rate, so if some of your missed calls are spam, vendors or existing customers, count only the ones that could have turned into work.

How the missed call math works

The calculator uses one simple formula, which is your missed calls per week times 52 weeks, times your close rate, times your average job value. With the default numbers, that's 3 × 52 × 20% × $1,500, which comes out to $46,800 a year.

Missed calls per week × 52 × close rate × average job = estimated revenue lost per year.

The jobs number is the same math without the dollar amount at the end, so 3 missed calls a week at a 20% close rate works out to about 31 jobs a year that may have gone to somebody else. Your close rate here means the share of real callers who usually end up hiring you, so if you win about 1 out of every 5 people who call for a quote, that's 20%.

The number is an estimate, and it leans on two assumptions you should keep in mind. It assumes each missed call was a real customer, and it assumes none of them would have called you back. Some people do leave a voicemail or try again, but when Quo looked at its call data, only about 15% of callers left a voicemail when nobody picked up. In a 2025 CallRail survey of 1,000 U.S. consumers, 78% said they had given up on a business after an unanswered call and 21% said they immediately call another business instead.

The calculator also leaves out repeat work and referrals, so for a lot of contractors the real cost of a lost customer is higher than the first job.

What's a typical missed call rate for contractors?

There isn't one reliable industry number, because it depends on your trade, your crew size and how busy you are, so the best number to use is your own. Scroll back through your phone's call log for the last two weeks, count the calls you didn't answer from numbers you didn't know, and divide by two to get your missed calls per week.

How does missed-call text-back work?

When a call to your business number goes unanswered, the system sends the caller an automatic text from that same number a few seconds later, saying you got the call and asking what they need.
