Pricing FAQ

Questions about the plans

Still deciding? Call or text and we'll walk you through it.

Is there a contract?

No. Every plan is month to month, so you stay because it's working.

How does yearly billing work?

Pay for 10 months upfront and get 12. That's 2 months free on any plan.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Start with Website and move up to All-In-One whenever you're ready. Your site stays the same.

Who pays for the Google Ads?

You do, directly to Google from your own account. Our fee covers setting up and managing the ads.

How long does setup take?

Your website is built in 2 to 5 days and the full All-In-One system in 7 to 10, and the clock starts once you've sent us everything we need.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo (or we'll make one), photos of your work, your services and the areas you cover. We handle the rest.

Who owns my domain name?

We buy your domain and cover it as part of your plan. It's registered for your business, and if you ever leave, we transfer it to you.

Can I keep my business phone number?

Yes. Missed-call text-back and spam blocking work with the number your customers already call.
