How the plans work

Meet Melissa.

Let's say someone in your area needs exactly what you do. Her name is Melissa. She opens Google, searches for the service she needs, and starts comparing a few local businesses.

Stage 1

Essentials

$147/month

When Melissa finds you, she lands on a real website that shows what you do, the towns you work in, photos of your jobs and your reviews.

She taps to call while you're up on a ladder with your hands full, so the call goes to voicemail. Normally that's where you lose her, because she has three or four other contractors sitting right in front of her and the next one is one tap away.

With Essentials she gets a text back from your business number within seconds, so the conversation keeps going until you can pick it up. When the job is done she gets a text asking for a review, and you can hand her a card with a QR code that takes her straight to it.

Melissa finds you, trusts you, and hears back even when you can't pick up.

So the calls are covered. What about everything else that comes at you in a week?
Stage 2

All In One

$279/month

Melissa isn't the only one trying to reach you. Some people would rather type a question into the chat on your site at 9 at night, some message you on Facebook or Instagram, and a good share of your calls are robots trying to sell you something.

All In One puts every real message in one inbox on your phone and screens the spam calls out before they ring. Customers can book a time on your calendar and get reminders, so they actually show up.

Your reviews get answered for you, and if a low one comes in you get a text right away with a reply already drafted. Happy customers get asked for a referral, past customers get a text when their season comes around, and we clean up your Google Business Profile so it matches your site.

The jobs you already finished keep bringing you reviews, referrals and repeat work.

Now the system is working. So how do we get more Melissas into it?

Pricing FAQ

Questions about the plans

Still deciding? Call or text and we'll walk you through it.

Is there a contract?

There is no contract on a monthly plan, so you stay because it's working.

Is there a setup fee?

Monthly plans have a one-time $297 setup fee on the first payment, which covers building your site and setting up your business number and texting. Quarterly and yearly plans have no setup fee.

How do quarterly and yearly billing work?

Quarterly is one payment every 3 months. Yearly is 10 months paid up front for 12 months of service, which works out to 2 months free.

Can I switch plans later?

You can start with Essentials and move up to All In One whenever you're ready, and your site stays the same.

How long does setup take?

Your website is built in 2 to 5 days and the full system in 7 to 10, and the clock starts once you've sent us everything we need.

What do I need to provide?

We need your logo (or we'll make one), photos of your work, your services, the towns you cover, and the business details the phone carriers ask for before you can text customers. We handle the rest.

Who owns my domain name?

We buy your domain and cover it as part of your plan. It's registered for your business, and if you ever leave, we transfer it to you.

What happens with my phone number?

Every plan comes with a local business number in your area code that rings the phone you already carry, and that number is what makes missed-call text back work. Your current number stays yours.
