How to Give Build Boosters Access to Your Google Business Profile

A 2-minute walkthrough for adding us as a manager on your Google profile. You stay the owner the whole time, and you can remove us whenever you want.

By Madeleine JolieNovember 12, 20263 min read
The short answer

Open your Google Business Profile (search your business name on Google while signed in, or go to business.google.com). Open the menu, choose Business Profile settings, then People and access. Click Add, type the email address we gave you, set the role to Manager, and click Invite. That's it. You stay the owner, and you can remove our access at any time.

To set up your reviews, photos, posts and missed-call text-back, we need access to your Google Business Profile. You don't need to share your password, and you don't hand over ownership. Google lets you invite us as a Manager, which gives us what we need to do the work while you stay fully in control.

Before you start: sign in to the Google account that owns your business profile. If you're not sure which one that is, it's usually the email you used when you first set up the listing.

On a computer

  1. Open your profile. Go to business.google.com, or search your business name on Google while you're signed in. Your profile tools show up at the top of the results.
  2. Open the menu. Click the three dots (⋮) or More, then choose Business Profile settings.
  3. Go to People and access. This is where you see everyone who can manage your profile.
  4. Click Add. Look for the add icon or Invite new users at the top.
  5. Enter our email. Type the email address we sent you during onboarding.
  6. Choose Manager. Under Access, pick Manager, not Owner.
  7. Click Invite. We'll get an email, accept it, and let you know once we're in.

On your phone

  1. Open the Google app or Google Maps and make sure you're signed in to the account that owns the profile.
  2. Search your business name, or tap your profile picture and choose Your Business Profile.
  3. Tap the three dots (⋮), then Business Profile settings, then People and access.
  4. Tap Add, enter the email we gave you, choose Manager, and tap Invite.

Google moves buttons around from time to time, so the exact wording on your screen might be slightly different. If something doesn't match, text us a screenshot and we'll point you to it.

Why Manager and not Owner?

A Manager can update your info, reply to reviews, and add photos and posts. A Manager can't remove you, add other people, or delete your profile. Only owners can do that. Your business stays yours, and that's how it should be.

  • You keep full ownership of your profile
  • You never share your password
  • You can see everyone with access under People and access
  • You can remove our access at any time with one click

Don't have a Google Business Profile yet?

No problem. Let us know and we'll help you create one and get it verified. If a listing already exists for your business but nobody on your team can get into it, we'll help you request access from Google.

Troubleshooting

  • You don't see Business Profile settings. You're probably signed in to the wrong Google account. Switch accounts and try again.
  • You don't see the Add button. Your account is a Manager, not an Owner. Ask the profile's owner to send the invite, or to make you an owner first.
  • The invite says pending. That's normal. It stays pending until we accept it, usually the same day.

FAQ

Common questions

Have a question that isn't here? Call or text us at (786) 479-8856 and we'll get back to you the same day.

Do I need to give you my Google password?

No. You never share your password. You invite us by email from your profile settings, and we use our own Google account.

Can you remove me from my own profile?

No. As a Manager we can't remove owners, add other people or delete the profile. Only owners can do that.

How do I remove your access later?

Go to Business Profile settings, then People and access, select our account and remove it. It takes effect right away.

What if I don't know which Google account owns my profile?

Try signing in with the email you used when you set up the listing. If none of your accounts can manage it, we'll help you request access from Google.
