Privacy Policy

How Build Boosters collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated November 11, 2026

Who we are

This website, buildboosters.com, is run by Build Boosters, operated by Madeleine Jolie Stonbely ("Build Boosters", "we", "us"). We build websites and marketing systems for contractors and home service businesses.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to give us when you fill out a form, start a chat, call or text us. That can include your name, phone number, email address, business name, website and the details of your request. We also collect basic website usage data, such as pages visited, through cookies and analytics tools.

How we use it

  • To respond to your questions and requests
  • To schedule calls and send appointment confirmations and reminders
  • To send you information about our services, if you've agreed to receive it
  • To improve our website and services

Text messaging (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive text messages, we use it only to communicate with you about your inquiry, appointments, account and our services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business (such as our CRM, hosting and messaging providers), and when required by law.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to the people and tools that need it.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information, or stop contacting you, at any time. Reply STOP to any text to stop text messages.
