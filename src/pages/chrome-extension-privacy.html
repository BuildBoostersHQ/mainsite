Contractor Website Checker privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

The short version

The Contractor Website Checker does not collect, store, sell or send any data about you or the websites you check, and it has no account, no sign-up and no tracking.

What the extension does

When you click the extension's icon, it reads the page in your current tab, such as the headline, title, description, images, links, forms and structured data, so it can show you a score and a list of things to fix. That check happens entirely inside your browser, and the results are only shown in the popup.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. The extension makes no network requests, has no analytics, and doesn't save your results or your browsing history, so nothing leaves your computer and nothing is kept after you close the popup.

Permissions

The extension uses the activeTab permission, which lets it look at the current tab only when you click its icon, and the scripting permission, which lets it run its check on that page. It doesn't ask for access to all websites and can't run in the background.

Links to our website

The popup includes links to buildboosters.com. If you click one, you'll be visiting our website, which is covered by our website privacy policy.

Changes

If the extension ever changes how it handles data, we'll update this page before that version is released.
