Why Naples Homeowners Are Selective

A lot of Naples homeowners own high-value properties, and many are retirees who have the time to compare contractors carefully. They read Google reviews, look at project photos, check how long you've been in business, and visit your website before they ever pick up the phone.

If your online presence doesn't match the quality of your work, you lose to a competitor who looks more professional on screen. A clean website with real project photos, strong reviews, and clear pricing guidance tells a Naples homeowner you take your business as seriously as they take their property.

Seasonal residents add another layer. Snowbirds who own homes in Naples but live up north half the year need contractors they can trust to work on their property while they're away. They find those contractors online, often from out of state. Your website is their only window into your business.
