How Build Boosters Works: From Free Demo to Booked Jobs

No mystery, no 40-page proposal. Here is exactly what happens from the day you see your demo to the day your phone starts ringing more.

By Madeleine JolieNovember 12, 20264 min read
The short answer

It takes six steps. See a free demo of your site, pick your plan, book a 45-minute launch call, send us your logo, photos and details, then we build and test everything and send it to you for review. Most sites go live in about two weeks once we have your info. After launch, we keep it running, make updates for you, and keep improving it.

Most contractors have been burned by a web person who disappeared, or a marketing company that took months and never explained what they were paying for. We built our process to be the opposite: short, clear, and mostly done by us. Here's how it works.

Step 1: See your free demo

Before you spend a dime, you can see what your new site could look like. Type your business name and trade on our homepage and you'll get a live preview with your name on it in about 10 seconds. Click around, try the missed-call text-back, and share it with your partner. No phone number needed.

Step 2: Pick your plan

Start where you are and move up when you're ready. Your site stays the same when you upgrade.

  • Website: a professional site with service and area pages, hosting and updates
  • All-In-One: your website plus missed-call text-back, follow-up, online booking, review requests and one inbox
  • All-In-One + Google Ads: everything above, plus ads that bring new leads into the system

No long-term contracts. See current prices on our pricing page.

Step 3: Book your launch call

Right after you sign up, you'll book a 45-minute launch call with Maddie. We go over your services, the areas you work in, what makes you different, and how you want new leads handled. You'll also get a short website questionnaire. Answer what you can and leave the rest blank. We'll cover it on the call.

Step 4: Send us your info

This is the only real homework, and most of it you already have on your phone:

  • Your logo (if you don't have one, tell us)
  • Photos of your real jobs, your crew and your trucks. Real beats stock every time.
  • Your services and the towns you cover
  • Access to your Google Business Profile, so we can connect your reviews. Here's a 2-minute guide on how to give us access.

Easier to text it? Send it to (786) 479-8856 and we'll take it from there.

Step 5: We build and test everything

You keep running your jobs. We build your website, set up your follow-up, review requests and inbox, and handle the business-texting registration so your texts actually get delivered. Then we test it all: we fill out your forms, call and miss your line, and make sure every lead lands where it should.

You review before anything goes live. We send you the finished site to look over. Want something changed? Tell us and we'll fix it before launch.

Step 6: Launch and get booked

Once you approve it, we take it live. Most sites go live in about two weeks once we have your logo, photos and business details. From there, leads come in, follow-up runs on its own, and review requests go out after every job.

After launch: we keep it running

Your site isn't a one-and-done project. Need a new service page, a price change or new photos? Text us and we'll handle it, usually within 72 hours. We keep an eye on how things are working and keep improving it with you.
