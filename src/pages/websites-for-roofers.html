Where the work goes

Storm calls don't wait for you to climb down the ladder.

Roofing calls come in two kinds, and your website has to be ready for both of them.

The leak after a storm

A homeowner with water coming through the ceiling calls until somebody answers, so if your phone goes to voicemail, the next roofer on the list gets the inspection.

The full replacement quote

Replacement jobs move slower, because the homeowner is comparing three or four roofers, reading reviews and looking hard at photos of finished roofs.

The call you never knew about

When you're on a roof you can't answer, and the person who called usually doesn't leave a message, so you never find out that job existed.
