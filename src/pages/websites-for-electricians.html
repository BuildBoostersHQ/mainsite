Where the work goes

Electrical work runs on trust, and homeowners check you out first.

Electrical calls come in a few kinds, and your website has to be ready for each of them.

The outage or the sparking outlet

A homeowner with a safety problem wants someone today, so if nobody answers, they call the next electrician they find.

The panel upgrade or EV charger

Bigger jobs take research, because the homeowner wants to see your license, your insurance and reviews from people who had the same work done.

The call you never knew about

When you're inside a wall or up in an attic you can't answer, and the person who called usually moves on without leaving a message.
