Where the work goes

Lots of small jobs means lots of calls you can't always answer.

Handyman work comes in all shapes and sizes, and your website has to make it easy to say yes to you.

The honey-do list

A homeowner with five small repairs wants one person they can trust to handle all of them, so they look for someone with good reviews who answers fast.

The specific search

People search for the exact job, like drywall repair or a ceiling fan install, so a single services page rarely shows up for any of them.

The call you never knew about
